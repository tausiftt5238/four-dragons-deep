# Devlog: bigger spells, a fuller shop, and falling dragons

This one is mostly about how a fight looks and what you carry into one. Spells
look like what they are now, the higher ones especially. Orbs sell a lot more,
and charge properly for it. And a dragon that falls finally stays down.

## Spells that look their size

Every element has three rungs, Ember, Blaze and Immolate for fire and so on
for the rest, but until now they all looked the same: a few small motes over
whoever they hit. The first rung still does. The two above it now play their
own animations, from the same art packs as the characters:

- **Fire:** a fireball that flies in and bursts, then a column of flame.
- **Ice:** a star of ice shards that shatters, with a storm of motes on top
  at the top rung.
- **Thunder:** a bolt that drops and crackles up into a pillar of light.
- **Light:** a pillar of light with a ring at its feet, and a bigger one at
  the top rung.
- **Dark:** a pillar of violet shadow with a skull in it, and a bigger one at
  the top rung.
- **Physical skills:** rock spikes burst from the ground under the target.
  A plain sword swing is still a slash.

Healing used to show nothing at all. Every Cure now raises green crosses over
whoever it heals.

![The higher spells, cast in a fight](spells.gif)

Monsters play by the same rules. A monster's spell looks like the rung an orb
on that floor would sell you: from floor 6 its fire looks like Blaze, and
from floor 12 like Immolate. It's only the look; how hard it hits hasn't
changed. Attacks that sweep over your whole party, like a dragon's breath,
now show on everyone they reach instead of just shaking the portraits.

## Swords in one piece

The fight zooms in on every figure, and that zoom used to cut off anything
that reached past the middle of the frame. The hero's big swings lost the
ends of their arcs, and his fire slash lost most of its fire. Figures are now
drawn whole, so a swing can reach past its box.

![The hero's three swings, whole](swings.gif)

## A shop worth saving for

Orbs carry a lot more now:

- **15 new weapons,** and weapons can do something besides hit. The Venom
  Kris can poison, the Gorgon Spear can paralyse, the Dulling Mace can knock
  a foe's attack down a stage, and the Leech Blade and Vampire Fang drink back
  part of every blow. The Gambler's Knife hits for little but adds luck.
- **12 new armours,** with trade-offs: strength, magic or luck, up or down,
  and resistances bought with a weakness. Marsh Leathers keep poison off you.
  Mirrorplate and Dragonlord Mail are the deep end.
- **15 new supplies:** Tonics, Full Potions, Max Ethers, mists and rains that
  heal the whole party, the Megalixir, a Phoenix Plume that brings everyone
  back, draughts that raise a stat for the rest of a fight, and the Prism
  Stone, which raises every stat at once.

Prices go up with depth now. Gear used to cost 45 to 120 gold whatever floor
you found it on, and the deep stuff was a steal. Now each tier costs 100, 500,
1,000 and 1,800, and a scroll costs more the deeper its spell is. To match,
you don't start with nothing any more: Captain Ysolde hands you 500 gold from
the Watch's own coffers before you go down.

## Dragons fall

The dragon art packs never came with a death animation, so a beaten dragon
went on standing there, idling, until the fight ended. Every dragon now drains
to grey and crumbles into a heap of dust at its feet, and the heap stays.

## In the PC version

- **Strafing.** Q and E step left and right without turning, LB and RB on a
  controller. Both are in the button mapping in Options, so you can rebind
  them.
- **Whose turn it is** is marked by the same triangle that points at a
  monster, instead of a ">".
- **The battle cursor** is a box, like the menu's.
- **The message box** at the top of a fight is two lines tall, so a long
  message isn't cut off.
- **Long names** scroll like a banner instead of being cut off.
- **Pop-up windows** (level up, game over, chests, saving) sit in the middle of
  the screen instead of off to the right.

## Also in this one

- **The map** marks you with a triangle pointing the way you face, the same
  one the legend shows, instead of a dot and a line.
- **The turn icons** are now little Ghostfire heads, blue for your side and red
  for theirs. A half icon is the head without its flames.
- **The buff chevrons** under a monster sit lower, so they don't run into its
  chart.
- **In the Abyss,** the gacha table no longer promises monster tiers the Abyss
  doesn't have. It says "a monster" or "a strong monster", which is what it
  pays.

## Fixed

- **The opening scene** restarted every time you pressed Z, and after a few
  goes the game crashed. The New Game button kept the cursor while the scene
  played, so each Z started another one on top. That's gone.
- **Coming back from Options** on the title screen, the cursor was gone and
  you had to click to get it back. It now comes back to the button you left.
- **The "press Z to continue" hint** in the opening scene sat on top of the
  message box. The box is now big enough for its longest line, and the hint
  sits under it.
- **In the menu,** moving down onto Leave or Close lit up two commands at once.
  Only one lights now.

Thanks for playing.

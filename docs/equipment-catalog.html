Equipment Catalog

Everything the run could stock, laid out by the tier that gates it. Grey in game is what exists today; green new is a proposal; purple needs mechanic is a proposal that needs a new field in code before it can work. Mark each one keep or cut — the choices save in this browser, and the Copy button at the bottom hands me the verdict list.

Tier gating is cumulative, exactly as Weapon.for_floor() does it now: an orb on floor n stocks its own tier and every tier below. Drag the floor slider to see one orb's actual shelf.

Decisions

Open questions

  1. Elemental blades need a new field. Weapons today carry only str_bonus, mag_bonus and agl_pen. A fire sword means adding attack_element to a weapon and having a basic swing read it — demons already have exactly that field. The knock-on is the good kind: a free elemental hit with no MP cost, but swinging into a demon that drains or repels that element ends your phase. Yes to the field?
  2. A light-element blade can banish. Light and dark expel rather than wound, so the Argent Blade and Excalibur would give a plain swing a banish roll off luck. Bosses null both lines already, so it can't cheese them — but it would shred regular floors. Options: allow it, halve the roll for weapons, or keep weapons off the two banishing lines entirely.
  3. Gear prices are flat. OrbUI.item_price is 20 + floor * 25, so every tier-IV weapon costs 120 gold — Excalibur at the same price as a potion run. Gear wants its own multiplier, or a price field per piece the way the dispel scrolls have one.
  4. Excalibur on a shelf? Everything else here can be bought. A capstone sword reads better as a tier-IV chest or a floor-20 boss drop. Sell it, or find it?
  5. Accessories are never stocked. _stock() appends Weapon.for_floor and Armor.for_floor but not Accessory.for_floor — so the ten trinkets can only be found, never bought. Deliberate, or an oversight?
  6. A gating inconsistency. Accessory.for_floor compares the piece's floor against the raw floor number, while weapons and armour compare against the tier. So a tier-IV trinket like the Ferryman's Coin turns up on floor 4. Almost certainly a bug — want it fixed to match?
  7. Armour has no luck field. Fine as is, but gold and platinum plate would traditionally carry some. Leave armour to defence and agility only?
Thirty pieces of gear exist today — 12 weapons, 8 armour, 10 trinkets. This page proposes 28 more, for 58. Worth knowing before you keep them all: a floor-20 orb already shelves all 20 weapons and armour at once, and six rows fit on a page. Keep every proposal and that shelf becomes 42 rows, seven pages deep. Nothing here is written to weapon.gd, armor.gd or accessory.gd yet — cull first, then I implement only the survivors.

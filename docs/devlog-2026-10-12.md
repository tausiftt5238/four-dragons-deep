# Devlog: a name of your own, and fights that keep time

This one gives the hero a name, makes a fight's hits land when they should,
and gets the game ready for a controller: Steam Deck button icons, and a fix
for presses that counted twice.

## The hero gets a name

Captain Ysolde has always lost your name halfway through thanking you
("...uhhh... hero. Right? Yes. Hero."). Now, at that moment, you can tell her.

On a PC it works like naming someone in Pokémon: a board of letters, ten
slots, walked with the arrow keys or the d-pad. Z or A writes the letter
under the cursor, X or B takes the last one back, and Done finishes. The
name starts as her guess, Hero; the first letter you write replaces it.

![Naming the hero](name.gif)

On a phone the board would be too small to hit, so it's a text field, and the
phone's own keyboard comes up for it. The field moves up to the top of the
screen while the keyboard is out, so the keyboard doesn't cover it.

- **She uses it.** Write "Mira" and she says "...Mira. Right? Yes. Mira.
  That will do nicely." Keep Hero and the joke stays as it was.
- **It goes everywhere.** Fights, the party cards, the menu, and her lines in
  the Abyss all use your name.
- **It's in your save.** A save from before names existed loads as Hero.
- **Skip** goes to the name first, then skips the rest of the scene.

## Hits that land on time

The hit used to land the moment you chose an attack, while the swing that
threw it was only starting. Now the swing plays out first and the hit lands
at the end of it. The same goes for spells, monster attacks, and bosses.

![The swing, then the hit](hit.gif)

## The triangle follows your aim

When you pick a target, the triangle over the monsters now moves with the
cursor, so you can see who you're about to hit before you hit them. Back out
and it goes back to your actual target. It also sits on the monster's head
now, not on top of its frame, so a short monster's triangle no longer lands
on the monster standing above it.

![Picking a target](aim.gif)

## Controllers

Every button the game can show is now drawn the way the Steam Deck draws
them: a black button with a white letter and a shadow underneath. A selects
and B cancels, as on a Deck or an Xbox controller. The prompts, the button
mapping in Options and the hints in the dungeon all switch to these as soon
as you touch a controller, and back to key names when you touch the keyboard.

![The controller icons](pad-icons.png)

Running the game from desktop mode on a Steam Deck, every press counted
twice: one step became two, and one press of A could choose twice. Steam was
sending each press as the controller button and as a keyboard key. The game now
notices when the same press arrives from both at once and keeps one.

## Also in this one

- **Going back** is an arrow instead of "<", and the tutorial's Next has a
  matching one. They're bigger in the tutorial.
- **Lists wrap round** in the orb and in the menu: up from the top goes to the
  bottom, and down from the bottom goes to the top.
- **Long names scroll everywhere** they're shown, including the party cards
  in the dungeon. A Baby Green Dragon used to be cut off there.

## Fixed

- **The cursor disappeared** after a talk with a monster went wrong, and you
  had to click to get it back. It now always comes back.
- **The last item in a shop list** didn't show what it does. The page
  scrolled to its Buy button but not to the line under it, so at the bottom
  of the Trinkets the Tide Stone's line stayed hidden. The whole row comes
  into view now.

Thanks for playing.

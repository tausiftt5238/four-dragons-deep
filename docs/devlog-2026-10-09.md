# Devlog: the PC version is here, and the Abyss gets a bottom

Big changes this time. The PC version I'm making for Steam is on this page
now: the full game for Windows and Linux, and a free demo of it in the
browser. And the Abyss, the endless mode you unlock by beating the game,
isn't endless any more. It has bosses on the way down, and something waiting
at the bottom.

## The full PC version, for Windows and Linux

The whole PC game is up as a download for Windows and Linux, for $5, the same
price it will be on Steam. It's everything: all twenty-five floors, the four
dragons, the Necromancer, and the Abyss with its new bosses and its end. It's
played on a wide screen with a keyboard, a mouse or a controller, and every
button can be rebound.

## The browser version is now a free demo

What you play in the browser here is now that PC version, as a free demo. It
covers the first five floors, up to and including the Ice Dragon.
Beat it, and Captain Ysolde comes back up to meet you, thanks you, and tells
you where the rest of the Tower is.

The phone version is still here as the Android APK, with the whole game,
Abyss included.

One warning: saves from the old browser version won't carry over, because the
browser files them under the game's name, and the name has changed.

## The Abyss has bosses now

Every fifth floor of the Abyss is a boss: a corridor with an orb at its mouth
and a dragon at its end. There are ten of them, the adult and ancient dragons
from the same art packs as the bosses of the main game. Each run shuffles
them, so no two descents meet them in the same order, and none comes twice
before you've met them all.

The Abyss has one life and no saving, so they aren't built like the four
dragons of the main game. Each is as strong as the monsters on its own floor,
but alone, with three times the HP, two turns a round, and three times the
experience and gold when it falls.

## And it has an end

At Abyss 50 the last of them waits: the Ancient Multihued Dragon, standing at
the end of a pier over a pit, breathing fire, ice, thunder and the dark. Beat
it and the descent is over. The captain comes down for you, and it ends the
way it should. Records remembers the clear.

The Abyss's other floors now lay every trap they have, every floor: ice,
spark plates, lava and teleporters together, the way floors 21 to 24 always
have.

## The Necromancer has a new hall

The bottom of the main game used to be a corridor. Now it's a hole. A
walkway runs round the edge of a pit, and a stone pier reaches out over it
with the Necromancer at its tip, watching you come in on the far side. You
walk round to the pier and out along it to reach him.

## Dragons you can talk to, drawn their age

The seventeen recruitable dragons from the last devlog now look their age in
a fight. A hatchling is about the size of a bat, the young ones of a hound,
the juveniles of an orc, and the mature ones still tower over everything. In
the PC version they also stand on the floor now instead of hanging just below
it, and the battle room's floor runs back far enough that no one at the back
of a group looks like they're standing on the wall.

## Winning feels like winning

When you win a fight, your party jumps for joy. The Victory window comes up
over the fight instead of replacing it, so you can watch them do it.

## Also in this one

- **Light and dark** now hit one monster or all of them, never two or three at
  random. Winnow, Scour and Sunburst, and Cull, Reap and Harvest are gone. If
  you'd learned one, you have the matching all-targets spell instead.
- **MP coming back** shows as a blue number in a fight, the way healing shows
  green: from the Tide Stone, from Defend, from an Ether, from a leech.
- **Exit Game** is in the menu under System, for leaving without closing the
  window. It saves first.
- **The status screen** fits without a scroll bar, says XP instead of EXP so
  the bars line up, and shows each stat's final number.
- **A demon's skills** on the Party page aren't cut in half any more.
- **In the PC version's menus,** Z steps into a page like Right does, and
  backing out with X or Left goes back to where you were instead of the top.
- **The APK** is no longer a debug build, so the test keys and the line of
  diagnostics in the corner are gone.

## Steam

The Steam release is still where most of my time goes, and the store page is
being put together now. I measured how much the game needs: about 100 MB of
memory, and anything that runs OpenGL 3.3 is plenty, even without a graphics
card. So whatever you play it on, it will very likely run.

Thanks for playing.

Four Dragons Deep: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Four Dragons Deep is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. This page explains what it does with your information, which is nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your saved games and settings (volume, controls) are written to the game's own storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave the device, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

The game asks for no device permissions.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online features are added, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
